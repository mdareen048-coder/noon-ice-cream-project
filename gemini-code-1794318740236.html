The Wildest Flavour Ever

Egyptian street snack culture meets high-end dessert engineering. Unexpected? Absolutely. Delicious? Gamed awy.

The Inspiration: We took Egypt's most iconic red packet of chips—Chipsy Shatta & Lemon—crushed them up into golden salty shards, and folded them into ultra-creamy sweet vanilla ice cream spiked with authentic Egyptian shatta heat and a sharp lemon zing. It's chaotic, it's bold, and your tastebuds will never be the same.

🌶️

Shatta Heat

A spicy kick that starts smooth and sneaks up on you. Warm, bold, and unapologetically Egyptian.

🍋

Lemon Zing

Sharp citrus brightness that cuts through the creaminess and makes your mouth pucker in the best way possible.

🥔

Chip Crunch

Real salty Chipsy potato crunch folded into every single scoop. Textural perfection!
